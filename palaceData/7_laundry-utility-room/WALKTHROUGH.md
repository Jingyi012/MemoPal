# Walkthrough — Level 07: Laundry / Utility Room

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Washer top
You arrive at **Washer top**. Notice the compass here.
The thread circles compass like a cyclone.
Follow it until it brings you to **Control panel**.

## Step 02 — Control panel
You arrive at **Control panel**. Notice the compass here.
The thread forms wave patterns.
Follow it until it brings you to **Detergent bottle**.

## Step 03 — Detergent bottle
You arrive at **Detergent bottle**. Notice the compass here.
The thread drips like liquid.
Follow it until it brings you to **Drying rack**.

## Step 04 — Drying rack
You arrive at **Drying rack**. Notice the compass here.
The thread hangs like a clothesline.
Follow it until it brings you to **Iron board**.

## Step 05 — Iron board
You arrive at **Iron board**. Notice the compass here.
The thread pressed flat like a ribbon.
Follow it until it brings you to **Mop bucket**.

## Step 06 — Mop bucket
You arrive at **Mop bucket**. Notice the compass here.
The thread swirls in water.
Follow it until it brings you to **Floor drain**.

## Step 07 — Floor drain
You arrive at **Floor drain**. Notice the compass here.
The thread points like an arrow.
Follow it until it brings you to **Back door handle**.

## Step 08 — Back door handle
You arrive at **Back door handle**. Notice the compass here.
The thread tied like a knot.
Follow it until it brings you to **Door crack**.

## Step 09 — Door crack
You arrive at **Door crack**. Notice the compass here.
The thread slips outside, bright sunlight.
Follow it until it brings you to **Patio first step**.

## Step 10 — Patio first step
You arrive at **Patio first step**. Notice the compass here.
The thread lands on outdoor tile.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
