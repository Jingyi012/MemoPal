# Walkthrough — Level 06: Pantry & Storage

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Pantry shelf 1
You arrive at **Pantry shelf 1**. Notice the compass here.
The thread tied around a jar.
Follow it until it brings you to **Cereal container**.

## Step 02 — Cereal container
You arrive at **Cereal container**. Notice the compass here.
The thread pours like grains.
Follow it until it brings you to **Snack basket**.

## Step 03 — Snack basket
You arrive at **Snack basket**. Notice the compass here.
The thread braided into basket handle.
Follow it until it brings you to **Upper shelf**.

## Step 04 — Upper shelf
You arrive at **Upper shelf**. Notice the compass here.
The thread dangling like a pull-cord.
Follow it until it brings you to **Step stool**.

## Step 05 — Step stool
You arrive at **Step stool**. Notice the compass here.
The thread wrapped around stool leg.
Follow it until it brings you to **Storage box**.

## Step 06 — Storage box
You arrive at **Storage box**. Notice the compass here.
The thread slips under lid corner.
Follow it until it brings you to **Cleaning supplies**.

## Step 07 — Cleaning supplies
You arrive at **Cleaning supplies**. Notice the compass here.
The thread foams like soap bubbles.
Follow it until it brings you to **Utility door**.

## Step 08 — Utility door
You arrive at **Utility door**. Notice the compass here.
The thread outlines the doorframe.
Follow it until it brings you to **Laundry threshold**.

## Step 09 — Laundry threshold
You arrive at **Laundry threshold**. Notice the compass here.
The thread trails onto tiles.
Follow it until it brings you to **Washing machine lid/door**.

## Step 10 — Washing machine lid/door
You arrive at **Washing machine lid/door**. Notice the compass here.
The thread spins like a whirlpool.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
