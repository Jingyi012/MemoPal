# Walkthrough — Level 09: Garden & Backyard Center

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Small tree base
You arrive at **Small tree base**. Notice the compass here.
The thread wraps trunk like ivy.
Follow it until it brings you to **Birdbath**.

## Step 02 — Birdbath
You arrive at **Birdbath**. Notice the compass here.
The thread ripples across water.
Follow it until it brings you to **Flower patch**.

## Step 03 — Flower patch
You arrive at **Flower patch**. Notice the compass here.
The thread becomes pollen-like sparkles.
Follow it until it brings you to **Stone border**.

## Step 04 — Stone border
You arrive at **Stone border**. Notice the compass here.
The thread runs along stones like a fuse.
Follow it until it brings you to **Compost bin**.

## Step 05 — Compost bin
You arrive at **Compost bin**. Notice the compass here.
The thread peeks from lid like a tail.
Follow it until it brings you to **Garden tools**.

## Step 06 — Garden tools
You arrive at **Garden tools**. Notice the compass here.
The thread wraps around shovel handle.
Follow it until it brings you to **Wheelbarrow**.

## Step 07 — Wheelbarrow
You arrive at **Wheelbarrow**. Notice the compass here.
The thread piles like glowing soil.
Follow it until it brings you to **Shed door**.

## Step 08 — Shed door
You arrive at **Shed door**. Notice the compass here.
The thread outlines the door seam.
Follow it until it brings you to **Shed interior glimpse**.

## Step 09 — Shed interior glimpse
You arrive at **Shed interior glimpse**. Notice the compass here.
The thread points to a locked box.
Follow it until it brings you to **Lock close-up**.

## Step 10 — Lock close-up
You arrive at **Lock close-up**. Notice the compass here.
The thread aligns perfectly.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
