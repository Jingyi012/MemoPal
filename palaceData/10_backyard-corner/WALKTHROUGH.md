# Walkthrough — Level 10: Backyard Corner (Final Reveal)

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Backyard bench
You arrive at **Backyard bench**. Notice the compass here.
The thread draped over bench like a sash.
Follow it until it brings you to **Lantern**.

## Step 02 — Lantern
You arrive at **Lantern**. Notice the compass here.
The thread glows inside lantern glass.
Follow it until it brings you to **Wooden box**.

## Step 03 — Wooden box
You arrive at **Wooden box**. Notice the compass here.
The thread tied in a bow around box.
Follow it until it brings you to **Keyhole**.

## Step 04 — Keyhole
You arrive at **Keyhole**. Notice the compass here.
The thread becomes a thin beam entering.
Follow it until it brings you to **Box lid opening**.

## Step 05 — Box lid opening
You arrive at **Box lid opening**. Notice the compass here.
The thread lifts lid like a pulley.
Follow it until it brings you to **Inside glow**.

## Step 06 — Inside glow
You arrive at **Inside glow**. Notice the compass here.
The thread forms a map-like pattern.
Follow it until it brings you to **Compass close-up**.

## Step 07 — Compass close-up
You arrive at **Compass close-up**. Notice the compass here.
The thread compass needle points back toward Level 01.
Follow it until it brings you to **Thread loop**.

## Step 08 — Thread loop
You arrive at **Thread loop**. Notice the compass here.
The thread forms a full circle (completion).
Follow it until it brings you to **Memory crystal**.

## Step 09 — Memory crystal
You arrive at **Memory crystal**. Notice the compass here.
The thread crystal reflects tiny scenes from previous levels.
Follow it until it brings you to **Final wide shot**.

## Step 10 — Final wide shot
You arrive at **Final wide shot**. Notice the compass here.
The thread all motifs subtly present; thread is now stable and calm.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
