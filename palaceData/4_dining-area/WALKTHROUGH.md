# Walkthrough — Level 04: Dining Area

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Dining chair
You arrive at **Dining chair**. Notice the compass here.
The thread tied like a bow on chair back.
Follow it until it brings you to **Table edge**.

## Step 02 — Table edge
You arrive at **Table edge**. Notice the compass here.
The thread runs along edge like neon trim.
Follow it until it brings you to **Placemat**.

## Step 03 — Placemat
You arrive at **Placemat**. Notice the compass here.
The thread woven into placemat fibers.
Follow it until it brings you to **Plate**.

## Step 04 — Plate
You arrive at **Plate**. Notice the compass here.
The thread circles plate like an orbit.
Follow it until it brings you to **Cutlery**.

## Step 05 — Cutlery
You arrive at **Cutlery**. Notice the compass here.
The thread wrapped around fork tines.
Follow it until it brings you to **Water glass**.

## Step 06 — Water glass
You arrive at **Water glass**. Notice the compass here.
The thread refracts through glass into rainbow.
Follow it until it brings you to **Pendant light**.

## Step 07 — Pendant light
You arrive at **Pendant light**. Notice the compass here.
The thread climbs up cord.
Follow it until it brings you to **Sideboard**.

## Step 08 — Sideboard
You arrive at **Sideboard**. Notice the compass here.
The thread curls around drawer knob.
Follow it until it brings you to **Kitchen doorway**.

## Step 09 — Kitchen doorway
You arrive at **Kitchen doorway**. Notice the compass here.
The thread forms an arrow shape.
Follow it until it brings you to **Kitchen first counter view**.

## Step 10 — Kitchen first counter view
You arrive at **Kitchen first counter view**. Notice the compass here.
The thread lands on counter corner.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
