# Walkthrough — Level 03: Living Room Core

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Sofa armrest
You arrive at **Sofa armrest**. Notice the compass here.
The thread draped over armrest like a scarf.
Follow it until it brings you to **Sofa cushion**.

## Step 02 — Sofa cushion
You arrive at **Sofa cushion**. Notice the compass here.
The thread pressed into cushion seam.
Follow it until it brings you to **Coffee table**.

## Step 03 — Coffee table
You arrive at **Coffee table**. Notice the compass here.
The thread circles a cup ring mark.
Follow it until it brings you to **TV console**.

## Step 04 — TV console
You arrive at **TV console**. Notice the compass here.
The thread threads through cable channel.
Follow it until it brings you to **Remote control**.

## Step 05 — Remote control
You arrive at **Remote control**. Notice the compass here.
The thread wraps buttons like vines.
Follow it until it brings you to **Bookshelf**.

## Step 06 — Bookshelf
You arrive at **Bookshelf**. Notice the compass here.
The thread used as bookmark protruding.
Follow it until it brings you to **Curtain**.

## Step 07 — Curtain
You arrive at **Curtain**. Notice the compass here.
The thread pulls curtain slightly open.
Follow it until it brings you to **Sliding door**.

## Step 08 — Sliding door
You arrive at **Sliding door**. Notice the compass here.
The thread trapped in door track.
Follow it until it brings you to **Indoor plant**.

## Step 09 — Indoor plant
You arrive at **Indoor plant**. Notice the compass here.
The thread climbs stem upward.
Follow it until it brings you to **Dining doorway view**.

## Step 10 — Dining doorway view
You arrive at **Dining doorway view**. Notice the compass here.
The thread extends into dining area.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
