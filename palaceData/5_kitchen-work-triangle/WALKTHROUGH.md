# Walkthrough — Level 05: Kitchen Work Triangle

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Cutting board
You arrive at **Cutting board**. Notice the compass here.
The thread outlines a cutting path.
Follow it until it brings you to **Knife block**.

## Step 02 — Knife block
You arrive at **Knife block**. Notice the compass here.
The thread slips between knives.
Follow it until it brings you to **Spice rack**.

## Step 03 — Spice rack
You arrive at **Spice rack**. Notice the compass here.
The thread loops around jar lid.
Follow it until it brings you to **Stove knob**.

## Step 04 — Stove knob
You arrive at **Stove knob**. Notice the compass here.
The thread glows like heat.
Follow it until it brings you to **Frying pan**.

## Step 05 — Frying pan
You arrive at **Frying pan**. Notice the compass here.
The thread forms a spiral inside pan.
Follow it until it brings you to **Range hood**.

## Step 06 — Range hood
You arrive at **Range hood**. Notice the compass here.
The thread rises like steam.
Follow it until it brings you to **Sink faucet**.

## Step 07 — Sink faucet
You arrive at **Sink faucet**. Notice the compass here.
The thread twines around faucet neck.
Follow it until it brings you to **Sink drain**.

## Step 08 — Sink drain
You arrive at **Sink drain**. Notice the compass here.
The thread disappears into drain.
Follow it until it brings you to **Fridge handle**.

## Step 09 — Fridge handle
You arrive at **Fridge handle**. Notice the compass here.
The thread grips handle like a ribbon.
Follow it until it brings you to **Fridge interior glimpse**.

## Step 10 — Fridge interior glimpse
You arrive at **Fridge interior glimpse**. Notice the compass here.
The thread illuminated inside.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
