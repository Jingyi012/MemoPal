# Walkthrough — Level 01: Street & Curb (Outside)

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Curb & drain
You arrive at **Curb & drain**. Notice the compass here.
The thread compass tangled in a glowing thread caught on the drain grate.
Follow it until it brings you to **Mailbox**.

## Step 02 — Mailbox
You arrive at **Mailbox**. Notice the compass here.
The thread loops around the mailbox flag, pulling it up.
Follow it until it brings you to **Walkway tiles**.

## Step 03 — Walkway tiles
You arrive at **Walkway tiles**. Notice the compass here.
The thread snakes between tiles like a glowing vine.
Follow it until it brings you to **Front potted plant**.

## Step 04 — Front potted plant
You arrive at **Front potted plant**. Notice the compass here.
The thread wrapped around a leaf, tugging it toward the gate latch.
Follow it until it brings you to **Gate latch**.

## Step 05 — Gate latch
You arrive at **Gate latch**. Notice the compass here.
The thread knotted on latch, tiny spark.
Follow it until it brings you to **Doorbell panel**.

## Step 06 — Doorbell panel
You arrive at **Doorbell panel**. Notice the compass here.
The thread pulses in rhythm like a signal.
Follow it until it brings you to **Doormat**.

## Step 07 — Doormat
You arrive at **Doormat**. Notice the compass here.
The thread stitched into the mat pattern.
Follow it until it brings you to **Door handle**.

## Step 08 — Door handle
You arrive at **Door handle**. Notice the compass here.
The thread spirals around handle like a ribbon.
Follow it until it brings you to **Keyhole close-up**.

## Step 09 — Keyhole close-up
You arrive at **Keyhole close-up**. Notice the compass here.
The thread aligns with keyhole, glowing brighter.
Follow it until it brings you to **Door opening crack**.

## Step 10 — Door opening crack
You arrive at **Door opening crack**. Notice the compass here.
The thread slips inside into darkness.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
