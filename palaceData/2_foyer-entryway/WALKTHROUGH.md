# Walkthrough — Level 02: Foyer / Entryway

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Entry bench
You arrive at **Entry bench**. Notice the compass here.
The thread compass sits on bench, thread drapes to shoe cabinet handle.
Follow it until it brings you to **Shoe cabinet**.

## Step 02 — Shoe cabinet
You arrive at **Shoe cabinet**. Notice the compass here.
The thread peeks out between doors like smoke.
Follow it until it brings you to **Umbrella stand**.

## Step 03 — Umbrella stand
You arrive at **Umbrella stand**. Notice the compass here.
The thread winds around umbrella hook.
Follow it until it brings you to **Key bowl / tray**.

## Step 04 — Key bowl / tray
You arrive at **Key bowl / tray**. Notice the compass here.
The thread swirls like a mini whirlpool.
Follow it until it brings you to **Wall mirror**.

## Step 05 — Wall mirror
You arrive at **Wall mirror**. Notice the compass here.
The thread reflected as a doubled line.
Follow it until it brings you to **Light switch**.

## Step 06 — Light switch
You arrive at **Light switch**. Notice the compass here.
The thread sparks as if flipping on.
Follow it until it brings you to **Hallway runner rug**.

## Step 07 — Hallway runner rug
You arrive at **Hallway runner rug**. Notice the compass here.
The thread stitched along the runner path.
Follow it until it brings you to **Living room doorway**.

## Step 08 — Living room doorway
You arrive at **Living room doorway**. Notice the compass here.
The thread frames the doorway like a portal.
Follow it until it brings you to **Side table**.

## Step 09 — Side table
You arrive at **Side table**. Notice the compass here.
The thread loops around coaster.
Follow it until it brings you to **Living room threshold**.

## Step 10 — Living room threshold
You arrive at **Living room threshold**. Notice the compass here.
The thread stretches across, inviting you in.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
