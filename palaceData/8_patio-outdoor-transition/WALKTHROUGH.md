# Walkthrough — Level 08: Patio / Outdoor Transition

Walk slowly in your mind. In every scene, find the **golden compass** and follow the **glowing thread** to the next location.

## Step 01 — Outdoor table
You arrive at **Outdoor table**. Notice the compass here.
The thread coils around table leg.
Follow it until it brings you to **Outdoor chair**.

## Step 02 — Outdoor chair
You arrive at **Outdoor chair**. Notice the compass here.
The thread tied on chair slat.
Follow it until it brings you to **Potted fern**.

## Step 03 — Potted fern
You arrive at **Potted fern**. Notice the compass here.
The thread weaves through fronds.
Follow it until it brings you to **Hose reel**.

## Step 04 — Hose reel
You arrive at **Hose reel**. Notice the compass here.
The thread wrapped like a hose loop.
Follow it until it brings you to **Outdoor tap**.

## Step 05 — Outdoor tap
You arrive at **Outdoor tap**. Notice the compass here.
The thread splashes like water.
Follow it until it brings you to **Wall lamp**.

## Step 06 — Wall lamp
You arrive at **Wall lamp**. Notice the compass here.
The thread glows brighter under lamp.
Follow it until it brings you to **Stepping stones**.

## Step 07 — Stepping stones
You arrive at **Stepping stones**. Notice the compass here.
The thread hops stone-to-stone.
Follow it until it brings you to **Small garden gate**.

## Step 08 — Small garden gate
You arrive at **Small garden gate**. Notice the compass here.
The thread latches onto it.
Follow it until it brings you to **Grass edge**.

## Step 09 — Grass edge
You arrive at **Grass edge**. Notice the compass here.
The thread becomes a glowing outline of a path.
Follow it until it brings you to **Garden bed**.

## Step 10 — Garden bed
You arrive at **Garden bed**. Notice the compass here.
The thread disappears under leaves.
This is the final step of this level. Let the thread carry you into the next level.
